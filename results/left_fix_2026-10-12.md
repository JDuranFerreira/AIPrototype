# Bare 'left' departure fix (2026-10-12) — _LEFT_FIX campaign

## Summary
Fixed the "bare left" departure problem: sentences like "6 left", "some left" were not understood as departures because the word "left" sat in L (literal) position and was never treated as a departure verb. This blocked ~10 grade-1 rows (waiter, kittens, seashells, cherries, walked-miles, etc.).

## Diagnosis
Traced all 62 blocked/guess grade-1 rows. Sharpest cluster: "left" after a number (`6 left`, `some left`) had no verb frame — perception tagged it as L (literal), so the reader never acted on it. The departure meaning existed in the frame table (`left|*` with c=0.694) but was filtered out by the 0.7 confidence threshold.

## Fix
1. **Added `_LEFT_FIX` flag** (`storyreader.py`): enables bare `left` as departure verb
2. **Added departure logic in `apply()`** (before main filter, OUTSIDE WORDLESS block):
   - Detects bare `left` (not `left over`, not stative `is/are/was/were/be/been`)
   - Case 1: existing `N\d+` candidates → promotes `left|*` to high-confidence `left` departure (`cell-N1`)
   - Case 2: no candidates → creates departure from `sit.thing` (current topic)
3. **Relaxed confidence threshold** 0.7 → 0.65 to admit `left|*` (c=0.694)
